M.Sc. in Artificial Intelligence & Finance

The Master of Science in Artificial Intelligence & Finance is a one-year online postgraduate programme designed for forward-thinking professionals and graduates eager to shape the future of finance. Offered by the Jindal School of Banking & Finance (JSBF) at O.P. Jindal Global University (JGU), this interdisciplinary programme bridges financial strategy with cutting-edge artificial intelligence, machine learning, and data analytics.

Through immersive live sessions, real-world projects, and mentorship from global experts, learners gain the skills to build AI-driven financial models, automate trading systems, enhance risk analysis, and optimize decision-making in dynamic global markets. The curriculum blends academic excellence with practical innovation, preparing you to thrive in the evolving world of fintech, investment analytics, and digital finance transformation.
